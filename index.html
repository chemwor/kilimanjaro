Summary What it added up to

The ledger

Kilimanjaro is the tallest freestanding mountain on earth. No range holds it up, just 3,000 vertical metres of volcano standing alone on a plain. You walk through five climate zones to get up it: rainforest, heather, moorland, alpine desert, arctic. Four days out of the trees and one very long night, and you are standing on the top of a continent.
