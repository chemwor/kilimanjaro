What the air was doing to us

A pulse oximeter reading taken each day on the trek. At sea level you sit at 95–100%. The mountain took it from 89% to the 30s — and the 30s is where the summit stopped being a hike and became an emergency. Tap a point for that day.

What it added up to

The ledger

Kilimanjaro is the tallest freestanding mountain on earth — no range holding it up, just 3,000 vertical metres of volcano standing alone on a plain. You walk through five climate zones to get up it: rainforest, heather, moorland, alpine desert, arctic. Four days out of the trees and one very long night, and you are standing on the top of a continent.
