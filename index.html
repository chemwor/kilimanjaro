Summary What it added up to

The ledger

Kilimanjaro is the tallest freestanding mountain on earth. It stands alone on a plain, of volcano rising straight out of it with no range around it. I walked through five climate zones to get up there: rainforest, heather, moorland, alpine desert, arctic. Four days out of the trees and one nineteen-hour day, and I was standing on the top of a continent.
